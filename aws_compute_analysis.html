Architecture Definition

What Is an EMR Serverless "Cluster"? (Application Pool Anatomy)

Unlike EMR on EC2, EMR Serverless has no static EC2 cluster to manage. Its "cluster" is a logical Application acting as an elastic capacity pool that carves out isolated Spark drivers and executors per job run.

1. Application = Capacity Pool

Defines Spark version, architecture (x86/Graviton), VPC, and maximum vCPU/memory ceiling. Costs $0 when stopped with no warm pool.

2. Per-Job Micro-Cluster

Each StartJobRun dynamically acquires its own isolated Spark Driver + Executors, billed per second (1-min min) and released immediately after.

3. Pre-Initialized Warm Pool

Optional pool of ready workers kept warm so 15-minute SLA jobs launch in seconds instead of waiting 1–2 min for cold starts.

4. Evolution Without Rewrites

Start with 1 Shared App → split into Per-Source Apps (Producer Spokes) → graduate flat 24/7 pipelines to EMR on EC2 or EKS.

Key Cost Takeaway

Why AWS Glue 6.0 Becomes Costlier Than EMR Serverless When Running Many Small, Frequent Jobs

Even after AWS Glue 6.0's 30% price reduction ($0.308/DPU-hr vs. $0.44 on Glue 5.1), high-frequency, low-record multi-tenant workloads (e.g., 50–250 tenants running every 15 minutes with <50K records per run) cause Glue costs to climb much faster than EMR Serverless for three structural reasons:

1. Rigid 2-DPU Minimum Floor

Every Glue Spark job must allocate at least 2 DPUs (8 vCPU / 32 GB = $0.616/hr), even if a small tenant only needs 10K records transformed. EMR Serverless right-sizes down to a 1 vCPU / 4 GB driver + small executors (~$0.23–$0.38/hr), cutting wasted capacity immediately.

2. Cold-Start Overhead × High Run Frequency

At 50 jobs every 15 minutes (144,000 runs/month), paying 1–2 minutes of Glue startup/provisioning on a 30-second job means 65–80% of your Glue bill goes to waiting for workers. EMR Serverless pre-warmed pools start in <5 seconds, so you pay almost purely for real Spark work.

3. Shared Pool + No Account Job Caps

EMR Serverless packs many short tenant runs sequentially through a shared warm pool (preserving per-tenant Spark driver isolation) without hitting Glue's account concurrency ceilings—and when flat 24/7 volume matures further, EMR on EC2/EKS lowers unit cost even further with Savings Plans or Spot.
